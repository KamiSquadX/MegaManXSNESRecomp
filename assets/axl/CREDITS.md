# Axl donor assets

The sprites in `sprites/` are Axl's, copied unchanged from Mega Man X8 16-bit
(Zashiko + TagTeam mod, version "After 3.6"), `Axl_mod/Player/Sprites` and
`Axl_mod/Player/Projectiles`; `Axl_bar.png` and `lives_axl.png` are from
`Axl_mod/HUD`, and `Axl_pause.png` is `Axl_mod/HUD/Pause/Axl_base.png`. `giga_screen.png` is made from
`Axl_mod/Player/Effects/giga_attack.png` by `tools/axl/make_giga_screen.py`.
The sounds in `sfx/` are X8 16-bit's `src/Sounds/FX - Giga Crash Start 2.ogg`
and `FX - Giga Crash Shot.ogg`.
`tools/axl/make_axl_manifest.py` reads the animation timings from its
`axl.tres`; `tools/axl/axl_manifest.json` is the result.
